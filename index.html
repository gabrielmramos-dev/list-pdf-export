Questions

Is my ClickUp data safe?

The extension talks only to the official ClickUp API, using your own personal token, which is stored only in your browser. There is no server of ours in the middle: the PDF is built in your browser. No analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy.

Does it work on the free ClickUp plan?

It uses ClickUp's public API, which is available on all plans, including Free Forever.

Can't I just print from ClickUp?

You can: Ctrl/Cmd+P prints the List view as it appears on screen, after you scroll to the bottom so every task loads. If you need a clean document with subtasks under their parent, chosen columns and your logo, that's what this extension is for. The guide compares all the options.

Does it change anything in my ClickUp workspace?

No. It only reads your List. It never creates, edits or deletes tasks.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open the extension panel and click "Manage or cancel subscription". You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
