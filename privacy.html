Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

List PDF Export for ClickUp ("the extension") turns a ClickUp List into a PDF. Your ClickUp data stays in your browser. We do not collect, store, sell or share it.

What the extension handles

  • Your ClickUp personal API token. You paste it into the extension. It is saved only in your browser's local extension storage and is sent only to the official ClickUp API (api.clickup.com) to read your List.
  • Your ClickUp List data (task names, statuses, assignees, dates, priorities, custom fields, descriptions). It is read from the ClickUp API only when you click "Create PDF", is used only to build the report on your screen, and is not saved by the extension.
  • The address of the open ClickUp tab, read only when you click "Detect", to find which List you are viewing.
  • Your settings and optional logo, saved only in your browser's local extension storage.

Payments (Pro plan)

Payments are handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) and Stripe. If you upgrade, you enter your email and payment details on their pages, not in the extension. The extension contacts extensionpay.com only to check whether your Pro plan is active. We never see your card details. See ExtensionPay's and Stripe's privacy policies for how they handle your data.

What we do not do

  • We do not send your token or your ClickUp data to any server of ours. The extension has no server.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising, in the extension or on this website.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for any purpose other than creating your PDF and checking your plan.

Uninstall survey

When you uninstall the extension, a short optional form (hosted by Tally) may open asking why. Answering is voluntary. If you choose to leave your email there, it is used only to reply to your feedback.

Deleting your data

Remove the extension from Chrome, or clear the token field, to delete everything it stored. You can revoke the token at any time in ClickUp → Settings → Apps. To delete your payment account, contact ExtensionPay or us.
